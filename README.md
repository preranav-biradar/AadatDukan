## 🏪 About Appa Aadat Dukan

**Appa Aadat Dukan Pvt. Ltd.** is a farmer-first agricultural trading platform built on the core values of **Trust, Fairness, and Sustainable Growth**.  
We ensure farmers receive **fair prices** for their hard work while connecting their produce to the **right markets** efficiently and transparently.

> _“We provide fair prices and reliable support to farmers, helping their produce reach the right market.”_

---

## 🚜 What We Do

- Enable **fair and transparent agricultural trade**
- Support farmers with **proper grading & pricing**
- Connect farmers directly to **trusted buyers**
- Build long-term relationships across markets
